# Government scheme source coverage

The local corpus contains three official guideline/order summaries: two
central documents and one state order. The summaries keep the source's stated
scope and version; they are not substitutes for the original documents.

- PM-KISAN Operational Guidelines, revised as on 29 March 2020, as stated on
  the official PDF cover. The exact publication date is unknown. Its eligibility,
  identity and procedure details are historical; verify current rules with the
  official portal and relevant State/UT.
- PMFBY Revamped Operational Guidelines dated 17 August 2020. This is a
  historic version; current enrollment windows, crop/area notifications,
  documents and eligibility depend on the applicable State/UT notification.
- Telangana Rythu Bharosa Scheme 2025, Agriculture and Cooperation
  (Agriculture-II) Department G.O.Rt.No.18 dated 10 January 2025. The order
  states an effective date of 26 January 2025, assistance of Rs. 12,000 per
  acre per year, cultivable agricultural land recorded in Bhoobharati (Dharani)
  as the basis for eligibility, exclusion of non-agricultural land, inclusion
  of ROFR pattadars and DBT to farmer bank accounts. Its jurisdiction is
  Telangana only. The scanned Telugu order does not specify a farmer-led
  application route or document checklist; the entry records those details as
  unspecified rather than inferring them.

The state GO PDF was downloaded from the official Telangana Rythu Bharosa
GOs/Circulars listing on 6 November 2026 at 15:16:52.179 UTC. It is a two-page
image scan: pypdf 6.21.4 found zero extractable text, so the order was checked
from rendered page images. The publication date comes from the order itself;
the PDF's embedded creation date is not treated as its issue date.

All scheme records preserve source URLs and identity, issuer, jurisdiction,
official classification, publication/revision information, UTC retrieval time,
and structured eligibility, required-records, benefits, limitations and
procedure text. Local JSON ingestion is explicit and does not fetch sources at
startup. Source retrieval or refresh remains a separate operation.
